## Project Overview
Luxury Furniture is a contemporary furniture store founded in 1979 by Miss Oratile Clementine Seabela, with the primary aim of transforming the appearance of homes for homeowners, property developers, and interior designers. It offers high-quality, stylish, and reasonably priced furniture for both home and office settings. The company features an expensive range of products, including sofas, dining sets, bedroom furniture, office desks, wardrobes, coffee tables, and decorative accessories. Luxury Furniture strives to blend elegance, durability, and comfort to assist customers in crafting exquisite living and working environments.
## Website Goals and Objectives
The website aims to:

• Showcase the company's furniture collections professionally. 

• Allow customers to request quotations.

• Establish a strong online presence and increase sales through digital marketing.

• Generate customer enquiries through contact forms. 

• Increase brand awareness and customer trust. 

• Improve customer convenience by providing product information, pricing, and delivery options. 

## Features and functionality
• Home Page with featured products. 

• About Us page explaining the company's story and values. 

• Product Catalogue organised into categories.

•	Contact Us page with enquiry form, phone number, email, and Google Maps location.
